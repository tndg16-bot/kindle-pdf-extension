# 1. 🎯 Purpose & Core Value

## 📣 Problem Statement
"Doing tasks is boring, and focusing on one area makes me unbalanced."
The user wants to visualize their efforts and "growth" in a fun, tangible way to maintain motivation and life balance.

## 💎 Core Value Proposition
**"Your Life Balance = Your Tiger's Health"**
*   **Gamification**: Completing tasks gives XP.
*   **Visualization**: A cute tiger grows as you grow. If you neglect an area (e.g., Health), the tiger's stats reflect that.
*   **Mandala Logic**: Based on the 9x9 Mandala Chart to ensure holistic goal setting.

## 🦁 Metaphor
*   **Avatar**: A "Cute Tiger".
*   **World**: Chill nature scenes (Bali, Sunset, Forest).
*   **Growth**: Tasks -> XP -> Tiger Level Up / Stats Change.
