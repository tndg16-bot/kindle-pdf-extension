# 📋 Project Overview: Communication & Psychology Learning
**Methodology**: Naomix Style AI Accompaniment x AntiGravity
**Goal**: To master "Communication & Psychology" through a structured, AI-guided daily routine of planning, execution, and retrospective.

---

## 1. 🏗️ Infrastructure Setup (Obsidian)
We will repurpose or create the following folder structure in your vault to support this learning journey.

*   `📂 communication_psychology/`
    *   `📂 todo/`: Routine, Long-term, and Short-term learning tasks.
    *   `📂 retrospective/`: Daily and Weekly reflection logs.
    *   `📂 log/`: Raw notes, thoughts, and "Episode" recordings from your daily life.

## 2. 🔄 The "Accompaniment" Workflow (Daily Cycle)

### 🌅 Phase 1: Morning Planning
*   **Action**: You check `todo/` and define today's learning goal.
*   **AI Role**: I review your available time and energy, suggesting a realistic "Micro-Step" (e.g., "Read 2 pages," "Practice active listening in the 10 AM meeting").

### 🏃 Phase 2: Execution & Logging
*   **Action**: You learn or practice.
*   **AI Role**: You report "Done" or "Stuck" to me. I provide instant feedback or encouragement.
    *   *Example*: "I tried active listening but got interrupted." -> I suggest a counter-tactic immediately.

### 🌇 Phase 3: Evening Retrospective (The Interview)
*   **Action**: We conduct a "Hearing" session.
*   **AI Role**: I ask deep questions to solidify your learning.
    *   *Question*: "What emotion did you feel when you were interrupted? How does that relate to the 'Psychology of Control' we studied?"
    *   **Output**: I summarize this dialogue into a permanent record in `retrospective/`.

### 🌌 Phase 4: Launchpad (Next Day Prep)
*   **Action**: We decide tomorrow's task based on today's reflection.
*   **AI Role**: I update the `todo/` list for tomorrow, ensuring a smooth start.
